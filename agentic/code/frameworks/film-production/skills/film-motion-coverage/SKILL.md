# Film Motion Coverage

## Inputs

Read the shot record, continuity pack, verified source versions, selected audio, provider preflight, and remaining generation authority. Load `aiwg show template film-shot-record` and `aiwg show template film-generation-receipt`.

## Workflow

1. Confirm source-quality checks have passed for this operation. Blocking acceptance alone does not permit a degraded still to become a motion input.
2. Specify a connected start–action–consequence–end sequence with usable edit handles. Allocate time to meaningful actions rather than letting a small preparatory gesture consume the source duration. Keep camera reveal limits explicit.
3. Track object identity and count, hand occupancy, contact and support, attachment endpoints, reach, clearance, and travel through the transition. Specify what must remain fixed as well as what changes.
4. Apply the recorded speech mode. Drive visible speech from the selected performance through a capable adapter; internal thought, narration, and off-screen speech do not imply mouth movement in the visible character.
5. Submit the representative authorized take through the discovered adapter and retain its task ID. Reconcile ambiguous outcomes before resubmission; log the actual cost or mark it unknown. Evaluate this provider's result rather than assuming another model's draft predicts it.
6. Play the full clip and frame-step contact, identity, and occlusion transitions. Verify lip sync, causal coverage, camera motion, and preservation of exact-source overlays. A contact sheet cannot certify these properties.
7. Record useful source ranges and residuals. After two same-defect failures, diagnose and change the permitted method before additional attempts.

## Outputs

Produce the source clip, generation receipt, playback evidence, usable ranges, and updated shot state.

## Continue or hold

Continue authorized related shots after the representative method succeeds. Hold failed clips from final conform; do not disguise missing causal actions with a misleading completion claim or treat paid generation as motion acceptance.
