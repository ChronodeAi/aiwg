# Film Voice Cue Sheet

| Cue ID | Shot/time | Speaker/source | Exact text | Audio hash/take | Speech mode | Performance | Mix | Sync evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Voice identity/authorized source / punctuation and pauses / cadence/timbre/assertiveness:
Internal thought: visible mouth state, level/tone/distance treatment; compare dry and processed in context. Repeated echoes are a separate creative choice.
Spoken dialogue: visible articulation, interruptions and listening beats. Preserve approved voice take across picture revisions unless changing it is requested.
Foley: physical mechanism and its phases; ambience, music beat/drop, silence, transitions and tail.
Mix targets from actual delivery specification, dialogue clarity at intended playback size, clipping/loudness/phase checks, original stems retained.
