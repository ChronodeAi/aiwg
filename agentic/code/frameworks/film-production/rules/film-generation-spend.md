# Film Generation and Spend

## Scope

Applies to external media uploads, paid generation, retries, batch expansion, and generation accounting.

## Requirements

- Discover the installed adapter and inspect its current input, capability, status, and retrieval contract. Verify actual provider/account/project identity. A connection or subscription does not establish task suitability.
- Before submission, confirm current source versions, relevant quality checks, intended causal coverage, speech mode, acceptance criteria, and supported controls.
- Record a bounded estimate, currency or billing unit, attempt limit, and remaining authorized budget. Use the user's selected configuration; do not hardcode providers/models or require a premium tier as a quality remedy.
- Establish a representative capability test before expanding an uncertain method into a batch. Passing one model's draft does not validate another model's behavior.
- Keep sanitized request/response references, provider task ID, input identities, output identities, method attempt, and observed status. Never place credentials in receipts.
- After an ambiguous write or failed poll, reconcile the known task before retrying. A missing local result does not prove the remote job failed or was free. Poll the actual handle with bounded waits rather than submitting duplicates.
- Record actual charged cost when evidenced; retain estimates and unknown charges as separate values. Include failed and rejected attempts in accounting where charged.
- After two same-defect failures, diagnose and change the permitted method before further attempts. Do not evade provider safeguards or silently escalate spend.

## Required response

Continue within existing authority without repeated permission requests. Hold a dependent submission when spend/upload authority is missing, task outcome is unresolved, or source/capability requirements fail. Complete independent preparation and present the precise remaining boundary.
