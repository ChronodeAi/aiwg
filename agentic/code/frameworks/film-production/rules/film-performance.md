# Film Performance

## Scope

Applies to story proof, voice selection, dialogue revision, visible speech, sound design, and mix review.

## Requirements

- Mark cues as internal thought, visible speech, off-screen speech, narration, or nonverbal action. Apply distinct motion requirements: internal thought does not move lips; visible speech requires a matching visible performance aligned to its selected audio.
- Preserve approved line text, chosen voices, selected takes, and accepted effects. Keep originals immutable, version revisions, and diff the dialogue list during assembly so a picture edit cannot silently remove a line.
- Select representative performances in story context before expensive dependent picture work. Voice identity alone does not establish acceptable delivery, pace, emotion, or listener relationship.
- Direct tone through observable performance intent and compare dry/processed options in context. Use level, tone, and space intentionally; repeated echoes are not a universal indication of distance or thought.
- Build causal audiovisual transitions: a character hears, responds, and acts in the intended order. Do not substitute arbitrary attractive shot order for motivated behavior.
- Align sound to visible physical phases and plausible sources. Mechanical clicks, motor noise, handling sounds, and impacts must correspond to events rather than act as generic decoration.
- Listen against actual picture and in the final export. Check intelligibility, sync, unwanted artifacts, abrupt levels, clipping, and whether music/effects support the story. Waveform appearance and endpoint frames do not certify performance.

## Required response

Keep mismatched speech, missing selected lines, or causally false sound on hold for affected acceptance dimensions. Continue authorized correction and preserve unaffected performances. Record subjective decisions separately from observed technical defects.
