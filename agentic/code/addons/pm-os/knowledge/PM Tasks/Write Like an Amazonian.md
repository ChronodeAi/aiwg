# Write Like an Amazonian

Theme: GPT, PM Craft, Writing
Edition: 106
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/free-prd-world-write-like-an-amazonian
Source: https://twitter.com/nurijanian/status/1741521571464806504

**Objective:** Rewrite a piece of business writing following [the Amazon writing rules.](https://twitter.com/nurijanian/status/1741521571464806504)

**Estimated Duration:** < 15 minutes

**Materials Needed:**

- A piece of business writing.
- Access to ChatGPT Plus.

I’ve created this GPT based on the writing tips from Amazon:

![](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fbf5e9a6c-d498-4baa-ae71-adc05e5832e8_1000x1590.jpeg)

It has 2 modes:

1. ANALYZE: it will tell you what rules your text violates;
2. EDIT: it will rewrite your text according to the rules.

That’s it. Enjoy!
