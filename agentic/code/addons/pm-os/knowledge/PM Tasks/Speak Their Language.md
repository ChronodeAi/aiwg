# Speak Their Language

Theme: Product Marketing, Research
Edition: 101
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/land-your-dream-job-easy-goal-practice
Source: https://www.amazon.com/Creative-Blindness-How-Cure-remarkable/dp/0857197304

1. Choose a user segment and spend 15 minutes researching their language, including slang, jargon, and communication style.
2. Select a feature or aspect of your product.
3. Dedicate 15 minutes to crafting a title or message that provokes interest rather than just describing the feature. Focus on mood and intrigue.
4. Draft a brief plan on how to incorporate this language into your product's user interface or marketing materials.

If some companies are moving *product management* closer to *product marketing* (*looking at you, Airbnb),* then this will help you flex your skills at marketing copy. I'm not suggesting we’re all going to become product marketers - but this is your no-regrets career-protective action that is super easy to take today. I see zero downsides, do you?
