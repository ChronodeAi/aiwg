# Product Teardown

Theme: PM Craft, Product Sense
Edition: 103
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/pca-in-business-shreyas-doshis-pm
Source: https://jackiebo.medium.com/five-exercises-to-practice-product-sense-57b9104ef8da

1. **Select a Product**:
    - Choose a product you use frequently, ideally daily.
    - Ensure it's a product with distinct features and design elements.
2. **Initial Observation**:
    - Spend 5 minutes using the product as you normally would, noting your immediate impressions of its design and functionality.
3. **Feature Analysis**:
    - Allocate 10 minutes to list and examine each feature. List in a table.
    - Assess how each feature contributes to or detracts from the overall user experience and product goals.
4. **Design Assessment**:
    - Spend 5 minutes evaluating the product’s design elements (e.g., aesthetics, ergonomics, interface).
    - Determine how the design enhances or hinders usability and appeal.
5. **Comparative Analysis**:
    - Take 2 minutes to compare this product with similar products in the market.
    - Identify what sets it apart and areas where it falls short.
6. **Write a Quick Memo** from the perspective of a Product Manager, collating the notes and drawing conclusions from it. BONUS: share it online.
