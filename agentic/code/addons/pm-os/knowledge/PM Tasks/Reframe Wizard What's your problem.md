**Materials Needed:**

- A problem you’re dealing with.
- Access to ChatGPT Plus.

Reframing is a process of looking at a problem from different perspectives to find new solutions or to better understand the situation. Here's how I approach reframing based on the "Reframing Canvas: What's Your Problem?" guidelines:

1. **Frame**:
    - **What’s the problem?**: I start by understanding the problem at hand. This involves asking questions to dig deep into the issue you are facing.
    - **Who is involved?**: It's also important to understand who is affected by the problem and who has a stake in the outcomes. This includes considering all parties involved directly or indirectly.
2. **Reframe**:
    - **Look outside the frame**: This involves stepping back and looking at the broader context or looking at how similar problems are approached in different fields or settings.
    - **Rethink the goal**: Here, I consider whether the initial goal is the right one or if there’s a more impactful goal to aim for.
    - **Examine bright spots**: This means identifying and understanding what is working well in the current situation or in similar situations. This can provide insights into possible solutions.
    - **Look in the mirror**: This involves self-reflection or organizational reflection to see if internal change or change in perspective is needed.
    - **Take their perspective**: Considering the perspectives of all stakeholders, especially those who are affected by the problem. This can lead to a deeper understanding and more innovative solutions.
3. **Move Forward**:
    - **How do we keep momentum?**: Finally, once a reframe has been established, I look into ways to maintain progress towards addressing the problem. This could involve setting new goals, establishing checkpoints, or considering new methods to keep everyone engaged and motivated.
