# Switch Between Tasks/Meetings Better

Theme: Productivity
Edition: 101
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/land-your-dream-job-easy-goal-practice
Source: https://share.snipd.com/snip/9ac3ed3b-3d20-4397-b348-aa5bb9f5f7fe

**Prep**

- At the start of your day, grab a piece of paper or open a quick Google doc.
- List your tasks & calendar events and identify moments of transition - moments when you have to go from one meeting to another, for example.
- Schedule/plan for short transition periods (about 15 seconds to 2 minutes) between tasks. Include these in your calendar or to-do list.

**During**

- When the time comes to switch tasks, consciously engage in a designated transition period.
    - Use this time to mentally disengage from the previous task (“I’m done with [meeting A]").
    - Reflect briefly (mentally) on what was accomplished. Don’t get stuck on imperfections.
- Acknowledge the transition, understanding that your brain is shifting focus (“I’m going into [meeting B]”).
    - Allow yourself a few minutes to fully immerse, remember context, ideas - but don’t dwell on any potential negatives.
    - Accept that deep focus may take a moment to achieve (“Take your time to focus.”)

**After**

- At the end of the day, in the same document, reflect on the effectiveness of these transition periods.
- Note any improvements in task engagement or overall productivity.
- Adjust the length and nature of transitions for the following day based on your observations (or decide not to do it again).

This practice aims to optimize your cognitive flexibility, enhancing your efficiency in task management. It's a blend of self-awareness and structured routine.
