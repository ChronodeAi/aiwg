# Active Interviewing: Researching Interviewers for a Product Manager's Job Interview

Theme: Job Interview
Edition: 103
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/pca-in-business-shreyas-doshis-pm
Source: https://share.snipd.com/episode/5d581a78-c440-4665-af1a-cd963571498f

- Contact the recruiter or hiring manager to obtain the names of the interviewers a few days before the interview.
- LinkedIn Profile Analysis:
    - Review career paths, roles, companies, common connections, and endorsements.
    - Note any shared educational backgrounds or professional interests.
- Google Search for Public Presence:
    - Search for interviews, articles, blog posts, or presentations by the interviewers.
    - Identify key professional interests and industry perspectives. Look for their socials. Instagram gives away a lot of interests.
- Company Website and News Review:
    - Examine the company's recent press releases or news.
    - Look for connections to the interviewers’ roles or departments.
- Develop Conversation Starters:
    - Prepare two to three topics related to the interviewers' interests or industry trends.
    - Prioritize these for engaging and informed small talk.
- Formulate Strategic Interview Questions:
    - Create questions linking to the interviewers' experiences or departmental challenges.
    - Align these with your skills and potential contributions in this new role.
