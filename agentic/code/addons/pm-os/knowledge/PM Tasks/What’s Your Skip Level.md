# What’s Your Skip Level

Theme: Career
Edition: 100
In the newsletter: https://www.nurijanian.substack.com/i/137737676/whats-your-skip-level
Source: https://share.snipd.com/snip/c6485885-670d-40ab-9ba0-12638339b1f1

Create a document and list down the five career buckets:

1. Head of Product
2. General Manager
3. Founder
4. Investor
5. Advisor

Quickly jot down your initial thoughts on each bucket. Note down how much each appeals to you on a scale of 1-10.
