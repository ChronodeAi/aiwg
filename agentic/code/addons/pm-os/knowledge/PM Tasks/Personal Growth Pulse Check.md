# Personal Growth Pulse Check

Theme: Career
Edition: 100
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/i/137737676/personal-growth-pulse-check
Source: https://share.snipd.com/snip/18cb4404-e78a-4ab1-bc88-34dd9e1b1dde

Open a new spreadsheet.

1. Column A: List down projects or tasks you’ve worked on in the last 3 months.
2. Column B: Indicate if each project had moments that stretched you ("Yes" or "No").
3. Column C: Describe briefly what made you feel stretched or uncomfortable.
4. Column D: Note what new skill or insight you gained from that experience.
5. Column E: Rate the growth impact on a scale of 1-5 (5 being highly impactful).

Calculate the average growth impact score at the bottom.

This spreadsheet serves as a self-audit tool to quantify and assess whether you're growing in your role. The higher the average score and the more 'Yes' entries in Column B, the more you're likely growing. If the sheet looks sparse, it's a signal to seek more challenging opportunities.
