# Simple, Pragmatic Vision Crafting

Theme: PM Craft, Vision
Edition: 102
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/ultimate-guide-to-opportunity-solution
Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/jobs-of-product-manager

1. **Preparation (5 minutes):**
    - Gather necessary materials: notebook, pen, or a digital document.
    - Find a quiet, undisturbed space for deep thinking.
2. **Review Current Vision and Company Goals (5 minutes):**
    - Briefly review the existing vision statement of the product (if available).
    - Skim through the company's overarching goals and objectives to ensure alignment.
3. **Reflect on Product's Core Value (5 minutes):**
    - Contemplate the unique value the product offers or aims to offer to its customers.
    - Consider what makes the product stand out in the market.
4. **Identify Long-Term Goals (5 minutes):**
    - Jot down where you see the product in 3-5 years.
    - Think about the technological, market, and customer behavior trends that might influence the product's future.
5. **Articulate Vision Statement (5 minutes):**
    - Draft a concise, inspiring vision statement that encapsulates the long-term goals and core value of the product.
    - Explain how it aligns with the company's goals and resonates with the product's unique selling propositions.
6. **Refine and Finalize (5 minutes):**
    - Refine it for clarity, inspiration, and alignment with company objectives *(hint: use the Flesch-Kincaid GPT I shared earlier)*
