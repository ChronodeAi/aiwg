# Cross Training Product Sense

Theme: PM Craft, Product Sense
Edition: 102
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/ultimate-guide-to-opportunity-solution
Source: https://jackiebo.medium.com/five-exercises-to-practice-product-sense-57b9104ef8da

- Quickly list down products from diverse categories.
- Randomly select two and brainstorm how ideas from one product could enhance the other.
- Focus on creativity, not feasibility, during this brainstorming.

You can use ChatGPT to get started, but I urge you to do this yourself.

ChatGPT can weed out some basic ideas out of your system, and you can build on top of those.

*You can also use my Edward De Bono GPT to come up with truly provocative ideas:*

[**🔨 Edward De Bono GPT: Maximise Creativity With AI**]({{https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/edward-de-bono-gpt-maximise-creativity}})

Let’s take Slack & Figma for example, here are some ideas I came up with on the spot:

- Slack slash commands -> Figma commands to quickly duplicate, rearrange layers
- Slack sorting unreads feature -> Figma showing latest unsaved file edits
- Slack polls -> Figma adding polls and embedded voting on prototypes
