# Progressive PRD System

Theme: Features, Productivity, Roadmap
Edition: 106
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/p/free-prd-world-write-like-an-amazonian
Source: https://twitter.com/sachinrekhi/status/1282347963759554560

### Objective:

To establish a progressive PRD system that organically grows feature ideas over time and a protocol for consistent, structured idea capture and initial PRD note creation.

### Duration:

30 minutes

### **Establish a Progressive PRD System:**

1. **Select a Digital Tool:**
    - Spend 10 minutes thinking about where you will store these documents. Evaluate based on criteria: ease of use, accessibility, integration with existing tools, and ability to grow with the project.
    - Decide on one tool to pilot the process.
2. **Define Contribution Guidelines:**
    - Dedicate 10 minutes to draft a clear, concise guideline document.
    - Include who can contribute, what type of content (ideas, requirements, designs), and when or how often they should review and update the PRDs.
3. **Communicate & Train the Team:**
    - Use the remaining 10 minutes to draft an initial communication plan.
    - Outline how you will introduce the team to the new tool and guidelines.
    - Plan a brief training or demonstration session to ensure everyone is comfortable with the new process.

### **Idea Capture and Initial PRD Note Creation:**

1. **Develop Idea Capture Protocol:**
    - Spend 10 minutes defining when (e.g., during meetings, after customer feedback) and how (e.g., directly in the tool, via a specific form) ideas are captured.
2. **Create PRD Note Template:**
    - Dedicate another 10 minutes to create a template for initial PRD notes.
    - Include sections for the feature description, bullet points for initial ideas, user stories, and any known constraints or technical considerations.
    - Make the template accessible and easy to duplicate within the chosen digital tool.
3. **Pilot and Iterate:**
    - Use the final 10 minutes to introduce the first feature idea into the system as a pilot.
    - Document a feature idea yourself, using the template and protocol you've set up.
    - Gather initial feedback on the process from a small group of team members and plan to iterate on the template and guidelines based on this feedback.

### Outcome:

All your ideas can now grow more context over time, and when time comes for roadmapping, you will have a cache of great ideas ready to validate and develop.
