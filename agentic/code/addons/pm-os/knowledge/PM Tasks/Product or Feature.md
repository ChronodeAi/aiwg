# Product or Feature?

Theme: Features
Edition: 100
In the newsletter: https://nurijanian.substack.com/i/137737676/product-or-feature
Source: https://www.mironov.com/feature/

Conduct a quick audit of an upcoming feature or product in your pipeline. Use a simple checklist:

- Can it sell separately?
- Does it have a separate customer ROI?
- Is it dependent on other products?

Compare the answers against your initial intent for this development. If there's a mismatch, schedule a meeting to revisit the strategy.
