# PM Resources

This folder contains 400+ curated product management resources including newsletter archives, articles, tools, books, and AI assistants.

## Folder Structure

### Lenny-Newsletter/ (358 articles)
Archive of Lenny Racitsky's product management newsletter - one of the most valuable PM resources available.

**Organized into 3 collections**:

1. **`becoming-a-senior-pm/`** (21 articles)
   - Series on advancing to senior PM roles
   - Leadership skills, stakeholder management, strategic thinking
   - Career progression and influence

2. **`building-early-stage-startup/`** (47 articles)
   - Series on 0-to-1 product development
   - Finding PMF, early customer discovery, iteration speed
   - Startup-specific PM challenges

3. **`all-posts/`** (290 articles)
   - Full newsletter archive on diverse PM topics
   - Product strategy, growth, metrics, hiring, team management
   - Case studies from leading tech companies

**Topics covered**: Product-market fit, growth loops, North Star metrics, OKRs, user research, pricing, roadmapping, hiring, team dynamics, stakeholder management, product strategy, experimentation, analytics, and much more.

**How to use**: Search for specific topics when facing challenges. Each article includes frameworks, examples, and actionable advice.

These resources provide the **knowledge base** that informs:
1. **Frameworks** - Practical tools derived from these concepts
2. **Skills** - Exercises to practice the techniques
3. **Templates** - Documents structured around best practices
4. **Workflow commands** - Multi-step processes combining skills (run via plugin slash commands)

**Usage pattern**: Read resources → Apply frameworks → Practice with skills → Document with templates → Run workflow commands for complex tasks.
