# Custom GPTs for PM Work

11 ChatGPT custom GPTs organized by where they fit in your PM workflow. Use them as optional sense-checks or companions — not replacements for thinking.

## Strategy and planning

| GPT | When to use | Pairs with |
|-----|-------------|------------|
| [Discover your strategy with Richard Rumelt](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-hiers5c7f-the-strategy-process-by-richard-rumelt?model=gpt-4o) | After completing a strategy kernel (diagnosis → guiding policy → actions) to stress-test your logic | `plugins/pm-workflows/commands/workflows/core-strategy-development.md` |
| [Make a plan on a page like John Cutler](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-HbtS0fCku-plan-on-a-page-maker-for-product-managers?model=gpt-4o) | When you need to compress a strategy or roadmap into a single-page summary | `plugins/pm-workflows/commands/workflows/core-strategy-development.md`, roadmap frameworks in `Frameworks/build/` |
| [TAM SAM SOM calculator](https://chatgpt.com/g/g-Yxxk2jO8i-tam-sam-som-calculator-for-your-startup?model=gpt-4o) | When sizing a market opportunity or estimating feature reach | `plugins/pm-workflows/commands/workflows/opportunity-mapping.md`, `PM Tasks/Rapid Impact Sizing.md` |
