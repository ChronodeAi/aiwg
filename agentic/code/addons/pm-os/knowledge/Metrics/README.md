# North Star Metrics

This folder contains 41 north star metric examples from successful growth-stage companies. Learn how leading tech companies define and measure their primary success metric.

## What is a North Star Metric?

A North Star Metric is the single metric that best captures the core value that your product delivers to customers. It's:
- **Leading indicator**: Predicts future success
- **Actionable**: Teams can influence it through their work
- **Understandable**: Easy to communicate across the organization
- **Customer-centric**: Reflects real customer value, not vanity metrics

## How to Use

1. **Find similar companies** to yours by business model or customer type
2. **Study their metric choice** - why did they pick this over alternatives?
3. **Look for patterns** across companies in your category
4. **Adapt, don't copy** - your North Star should fit your unique value proposition
5. **Test alignment** - does your proposed metric predict long-term success?

## Choosing Your North Star

**Good North Star Metrics**:
- Reflect delivered customer value (not just usage)
- Lead to revenue (eventually)
- Measure breadth and depth (frequency × engagement)
- Are influenced by most teams

**Bad North Star Metrics**:
- Revenue (lagging indicator)
- Registered users (vanity metric)
- Pageviews without context (doesn't reflect value)
- Metrics only one team can influence
