# Customer Discovery Interview Questions

This folder contains 100 customer discovery interview questions organized into 6 categories. These questions help you understand customers deeply through structured interviews.

## Categories

### 1. Competitive Landscape (12 questions)
Questions about competitors, alternatives, and how users evaluate different solutions.

**Location**: `competitive-landscape/`

**Example questions**:
- "How does our product/service compare to others you've tried?"
- "What do these competing products/services do better than us?"
- "Why did you choose your current product/service over alternatives?"

### 2. Customer Needs & Goals (21 questions)
Questions about problems, aspirations, challenges, and desired outcomes.

**Location**: `customer-needs-and-goals/`

**Example questions**:
- "What goals are you trying to achieve?"
- "What problems are you facing that hinder these goals?"
- "How important is solving this problem to you?"

### 3. Customer Profile (18 questions)
Questions about role, background, preferences, behaviors, and context.

**Location**: `customer-profile/`

**Example questions**:
- "Can you describe your role and responsibilities?"
- "How does a typical day look for you?"
- "What skills or tools do you use most in your job?"

### 4. Decision Making & Purchase Process (20 questions)
Questions about buying decisions, evaluation criteria, and purchasing behavior.

**Location**: `decision-making-and-purchase-process/`

**Example questions**:
- "What factors did you consider when deciding to use this product/service?"
- "Who is involved in the decision-making process?"
- "What would you need to see from a product/service to switch?"

### 5. Ideation & Feedback (10 questions)
Questions to gather reactions to proposed solutions and collect improvement ideas.

**Location**: `ideation-and-feedback/`

**Example questions**:
- "What is your initial impression of our proposed product/service?"
- "If you could change one thing about our proposed solution, what would it be?"
- "Would you be willing to try a prototype of our product/service?"

### 6. Product Usage & Experience (19 questions)
Questions about current product usage, satisfaction, and pain points.

**Location**: `product-usage-and-experience/`

**Example questions**:
- "Can you walk me through how you use this product or service?"
- "What do you like about this product/service?"
- "What do you dislike or what frustrates you about this product/service?"

## File Format

Each question file contains:
- **Question**: The main interview question to ask
- **Area**: The category it belongs to
- **Deeper Question**: A follow-up prompt to dig deeper into the answer

## How to Use

1. **Select relevant categories** based on your research goals
2. **Mix questions across categories** for comprehensive interviews
3. **Adapt the wording** to fit your specific product/context
4. **Use deeper questions** as follow-ups to go beyond surface-level answers
5. **Start broad, then narrow** - begin with profile/needs, then move to specifics

## Interview Best Practices

- Ask open-ended questions (avoid yes/no questions)
- Listen more than you talk (80/20 rule)
- Ask "why" to understand motivations
- Look for specific examples and stories
- Follow up on interesting tangents
- Record with permission for later analysis
