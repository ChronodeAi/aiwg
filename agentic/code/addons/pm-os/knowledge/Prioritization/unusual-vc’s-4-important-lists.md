# Description

Ultimately, whatever you decide to build comes down to these four buckets, which are what any product leader should weigh when making tradeoffs and defining priorities for the product roadmap:

## **List #1: Sales**

Are there product features your competitors have that you don’t that routinely cause you to lose deals?

Are there any product capabilities that prospects keep requesting in discovery meetings?

## **List #2: Customer Success**

Are there big, different pain points you are hearing from customers not solved by your current product/future roadmap?

Are there steps that your customers have to take before they can start using your product, or after they finish using your product, that you can improve?

## **List #3: Engineering**

Are there ways you could make your product faster?

Are there quality issues you’ve been ignoring or putting off?

## **List #4: Product Innovations**

What are the big, macro trends or shifts you can see coming in your industry/category? Are there any new product innovations that would enable you to “ride this wave”?

How can you expand your total addressable market (TAM)?
