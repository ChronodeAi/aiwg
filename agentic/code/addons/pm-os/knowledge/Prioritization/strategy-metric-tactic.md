## Description

The Strategy/Metric/Tactic method is a way of articulating and aligning product strategies, metrics and tactics. It helps product teams to define their problems, measure their progress and prioritize their projects.

The method consists of three steps:

1. Strategy: Identify the high-level strategies that will help achieve the product vision and goals. These are usually broad statements that describe what problems need to be solved or what opportunities need to be explored.
2. Metric: Choose a proxy metric for each strategy that will indicate how well the strategy is working. A proxy metric is a measurable outcome that correlates with customer satisfaction or business value.
3. Tactic: Generate a list of projects or experiments for each strategy that will move the metric in the desired direction. These are usually specific actions or features that can be tested and evaluated.

The article provides an example of how Netflix used this method to define their product strategy for improving retention in 2005. The article also links to other essays that explain how to identify proxy metrics and how to prioritize tactics.
