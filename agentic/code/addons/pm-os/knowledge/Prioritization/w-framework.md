# W Framework

Use When: B2B, Building Team Consensus, Prioritise Opportunities, Vision Setting/Long-Term Roadmapping
Author: Lenny Rachitsky
Don't Use When: In Delivery, Mid/Low Influence, Need Quick Decisions
Best for Prioritising...: Lanes/Themes
Artefact: Agnostic
Advantages: Full-Stack
Limitations: Draw The Rest Of The Owl🦉, Won't Help With Delivery

# Description

The W framework is a full top-to-bottom company planning process that helps teams align on their goals, strategies, and tactics for the upcoming year or quarter. It consists of 6 steps:

1. Define your vision: What is the ultimate outcome you want to achieve?
2. Set your objectives: What are the specific and measurable goals that will get you closer to your vision?
Identify your key results: How will you measure your progress and success for each objective?
3. Brainstorm your initiatives: What are the projects or actions that will help you achieve your key results?
4. Create a strawman plan: Take a first pass at developing the team plan based on your initiatives and available resources.
5. Get feedback, iterate: Share this early plan with the team, collect feedback, and iterate until you and the team feel good about it.
6. Finalize and communicate: Finalize your plan, communicate it clearly to all stakeholders, and track your progress regularly.

This framework might benefit anyone who wants to have a clear and structured way of planning their work and aligning with their team members and other teams. It helps product managers prioritize by forcing them to focus on the most important objectives and key results, as well as by providing a transparent way of evaluating trade-offs between different initiatives. It also helps product managers communicate their plans effectively to their team members, executives, customers, and partners.
