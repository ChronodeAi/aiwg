Use When: Building a New Product, Limited Influence, Need Quick Decisions, Prioritise Opportunities
Author: Luke Hohmann
Don't Use When: Internal Product, Vision Setting/Long-Term Roadmapping
Best for Prioritising...: Features, Initiatives/Epics
Artefact: Agnostic
Advantages: Lightweight
Limitations: Requires Your Goals First, Won't Help Set Goals, Won't Help With Delivery

# Description

Speedboat is a quick and easy way to identify what your employees or clients dislike about your product/service or what is impeding progress toward a desired goal. As individuals attempting to gain traction on products or projects, we occasionally have blind spots regarding what is impeding us. This game allows you to elicit feedback from stakeholders about potential roadblocks to progress.
