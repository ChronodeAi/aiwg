# Post PMF Strategy by Reforge

Use When: Established Product, Post Product-Market Fit, Growth Stage, Vision Setting/Long-Term Roadmapping
Author: Reforge
Don't Use When: Building a New Product, Pre-PMF, In Delivery, Need Quick Decisions
Best for Prioritising...: Themes/Lanes, Initiatives/Epics
Advantages: Comprehensive Coverage, Forces Strategic Conversation, Clear Bucket Categories
Limitations: Requires Confirmed PMF First, Doesn't Score Features, Won't Help With Delivery

Read Case Study: https://www.reforge.com/blog/product-work-beyond-product-market-fit

## Description

After initial product-market fit, the product work that got you here won't get you there. Reforge identifies four distinct types of product work that post-PMF companies need to manage simultaneously — and failing to explicitly allocate resources across all four creates strategic blind spots.

**The four types of product work:**

- **Feature Strategy**: Enhance the existing value proposition. Improve the core product for existing users. Incremental but compounding.
- **Growth Strategy**: Maximize the value proposition. Get more people to the value, faster. Acquisition, activation, retention, monetization.
- **PMF Expansion Strategy**: Add a new value proposition. Expand into adjacent user segments or use cases. This is how you find your next S-curve.
- **Scaling Strategy**: Enable the value proposition at scale. Infrastructure, reliability, support systems. The unsexy work that breaks if ignored.

**How to use it**: Map current roadmap items into these four buckets. Then debate whether the allocation is right for your stage. Most teams over-index on Feature Strategy and neglect PMF Expansion and Scaling until they become crises.

Most effective for product teams 12–24+ months past PMF, when the team feels busy but growth has plateaued and there's debate about what to prioritize next.
