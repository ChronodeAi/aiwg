# Description

Theme screening is a useful and easy way to figure out which themes or epics are more important than others. It can also be used to put whole projects or products in order of importance.

To use theme screening, you must first figure out what will be important to you when deciding which themes come first (or epics, projects, etc.). Some factors could be how important it is to current customers and how much money it will bring in for Q2.

**After you've decided on your criteria, choose a baseline theme. The baseline theme is one that is likely to be in the next release, but it's not a given.** 

For each selection criteria, all other themes will be compared to the baseline theme. 

So that these comparisons are useful, you need to choose a baseline that is likely, but not guaranteed, to be included. 

If, for example, you took the most important theme as your starting point and compared all the other themes to it, you would find that each of the other themes was less important. But you wouldn't be able to tell how much less important one was from the other.
