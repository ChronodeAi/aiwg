## Description

The Customer Problem Stack Rank (CPSR) is a data-driven approach to understand how important your idea is compared to the other problems your target customers experience. It involves asking your customers to rank the problem that your idea solves among all the other problems they face in a specific domain. This way, you can validate whether your idea addresses a burning pain point or just a mild inconvenience for your customers. CPSR can help you prioritize your product development and avoid building solutions that nobody wants.
