# Description

Aan opportunity solution tree is a product discovery tool that visually represents the link between three essential elements of product discovery and development: outcomes, opportunities, and solutions.

An outcome is a measurable change that you want to achieve for your customers or your business. For example, increase customer retention by 10%.

An opportunity is a problem or need that your customers have that could lead to achieving the outcome. For example, customers are not aware of the value proposition of your product.

A solution is a specific idea or feature that you can implement to address an opportunity. For example, create an onboarding tutorial for new users.

An opportunity solution tree helps you map out different ways to reach your desired outcome by identifying multiple opportunities and solutions for each one. It also helps you prioritize which opportunities and solutions to pursue based on their impact and feasibility.

To create an opportunity solution tree, you can follow these steps:

1. Identify your desired outcome and write it at the top of the tree.
2. Identify opportunities that could help you achieve the outcome and write them as branches below the outcome.
3. Generate solutions for each opportunity and write them as sub-branches below each opportunity.
4. Experiment with different solutions and measure their impact on the outcome using data and feedback.
5. Repeat steps 2-4 until you find the most effective solution for each opportunity.

Here is an example of an opportunity solution tree:

```
Increase customer retention by 10%
|
|-- Customers are not aware of the value proposition of your product
|   |
|   |-- Create an onboarding tutorial for new users
|   |-- Send personalized emails with tips and benefits
|   |-- Add testimonials and reviews from satisfied customers
|
|-- Customers are not engaged with your product features
    |
    |-- Add gamification elements such as badges and rewards
    |-- Introduce social features such as chat and leaderboards
    |-- Provide feedback and suggestions based on user behavior
```
