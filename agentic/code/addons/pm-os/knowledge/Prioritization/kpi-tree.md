# Description

A KPI tree is a visualization tool that shows how different KPIs (key performance indicators) are related to each other and to the overall objectives of a project, team or organization. It can help you align your strategies, methods and measurements with your goals.

Some benefits of using a KPI trees are:

- They can help you communicate your goals and progress clearly to stakeholders.
- They can help you identify gaps, conflicts and interdependencies between different KPIs.
- They can help you prioritize your actions and resources based on what matters most.

Some tips for creating KPI trees are:

- Use various types of KPIs, such as input, output, outcome and impact.
- Carefully choose your KPIs based on relevance, measurability and actionability.
- Revise your KPI tree as needed based on feedback and data.
