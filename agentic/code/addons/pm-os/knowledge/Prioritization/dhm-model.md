# Description

The DHM model is a framework for creating a product strategy that balances three elements: Delight, Hard-to-copy and Margin.

1. Delight: This refers to how well the product satisfies the customer’s needs and wants, and how it exceeds their expectations. A delightful product creates customer satisfaction, loyalty and word-of-mouth.
2. Hard-to-copy: This refers to how difficult it is for competitors to imitate or replicate the product’s value proposition. A hard-to-copy product creates a competitive advantage and a defensible market position.
3. Margin: This refers to how profitable the product is for the business, and how it contributes to its growth and sustainability. A margin-enhancing product creates revenue, cash flow and return on investment.

The DHM model helps product teams to identify their strengths and weaknesses, generate ideas for improvement, prioritize their initiatives and communicate their vision.
