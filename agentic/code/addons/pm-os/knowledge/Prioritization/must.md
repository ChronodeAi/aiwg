# MuST

Use When: B2B, Vision Setting/Long-Term Roadmapping
Author: Itamar Gilad
Don't Use When: In Delivery, Mid/Low Influence, Need Quick Decisions
Best for Prioritising...: Initiatives/Epics
Artefact: Diagram
Advantages: Full-Stack, Lightweight
Limitations: Draw The Rest Of The Owl🦉, Requires Your Goals First, Won't Help Set Goals, Won't Help With Delivery
Read Case Study: https://itamargilad.com/product-strategy-must/

## Description

The MuST framework, created by Itamar Gilad, is designed for prioritizing initiatives or epics in a B2B context when setting a long-term roadmap or vision. It is not recommended for use during delivery, when influence is mid or low, or when quick decisions are needed. The framework takes the form of a diagram and is described in detail in a case study, which can be found at [https://itamargilad.com/product-strategy-must/](https://itamargilad.com/product-strategy-must/).

The MuST framework is based on the idea of identifying and prioritizing initiatives that are most important to achieving a company's long-term vision. The name MuST is an acronym for "Mission, Strategy, Tactics," which are the three levels of planning that the framework helps to align.

At the highest level, the framework starts with the company's mission, which is defined as the ultimate goal or purpose that the company aims to achieve. This mission should be clear, concise, and inspiring. Next, the framework helps to identify the company's strategy, which is the approach or plan for achieving the mission. This strategy should be based on the company's unique strengths and competitive advantages.

Finally, the framework helps to identify the tactics or initiatives that are most important for executing the strategy. These initiatives should be prioritized based on their impact on the company's long-term vision and their feasibility in terms of resources and time.

Overall, the MuST framework provides a structured and strategic approach to prioritizing initiatives for long-term success in a B2B context.
