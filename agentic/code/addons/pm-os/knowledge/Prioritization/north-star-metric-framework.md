# Description

The North Star Metric framework is a model for managing products by identifying a single, crucial metric that best captures the core value that your product delivers to your customers. This metric is called the North Star Metric (NSM) and it helps you align your team around a common goal and focus on what matters most for your users.

The framework also includes a set of key inputs that collectively act as factors that influence or drive the NSM. These inputs are measurable and actionable variables that reflect the user behaviors or product features that contribute to the NSM. By tracking and optimizing these inputs, you can improve your NSM and deliver more value to your customers.

The framework helps you create a shared vision for your product, prioritize your roadmap, communicate your progress, and foster a culture of experimentation and learning.
