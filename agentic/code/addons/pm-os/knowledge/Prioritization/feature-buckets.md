# Description

A product manager can use the three feature buckets method for prioritization by grouping potential features into three categories:

1. Metric movers: These are features that directly impact the key metrics or goals of the product, such as revenue, growth, retention, etc. They are usually driven by data analysis and experimentation and are essential for achieving business objectives.
2. Customer requests: These are features that are explicitly requested by customers or users, such as bug fixes, enhancements, integrations, etc. They are usually driven by feedback channels and customer satisfaction surveys and are important for meeting customer expectations and reducing churn.
3. Delight: These are features that surprise and delight customers or users by exceeding their expectations or providing a wow factor. They are usually driven by innovation and creativity and are valuable for creating loyalty and advocacy.

The article suggests that a good product release should have some features from each of these buckets to balance the needs of the business, the customers and the product vision. The article also provides some tips on how to allocate resources among these buckets depending on the stage of the product lifecycle.
