Use When: Building Team Consensus, Building a New Product, Prioritise Opportunities
Author: Yoji Akao

Best for Prioritising...: Features, Initiatives/Epics

Limitations: Draw The Rest Of The Owl🦉, Need To Know Problems First, Won't Help Set Goals

# Description

Quality Function Deployment (QFD) is a method for prioritizing user interface improvements based on customer needs and business goals. It involves four main steps:

1. Identify customer requirements (what they want) and business requirements (what you want).
2. Translate customer requirements into measurable design characteristics (how you will meet their needs).
3. Evaluate how well your current and proposed designs meet those characteristics (how well you are doing).
4. Determine the relative importance of each characteristic and allocate resources accordingly (where to focus your efforts).

The QFD produces a decision matrix that looks like a house, with customer requirements on the left, design characteristics on the top, correlations between them in the middle, and importance ratings on the bottom. This matrix helps you visualize and communicate how your design decisions align with your customers’ expectations and your business objectives.
