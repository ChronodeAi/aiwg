# Prune the Product Tree

Use When: Building Team Consensus, Building a New Product, Need Quick Decisions
Author: Luke Hohmann
Don't Use When: Vision Setting/Long-Term Roadmapping
Best for Prioritising...: Features, Initiatives/Epics
Artefact: Diagram
Advantages: Lightweight
Limitations: Draw The Rest Of The Owl🦉, Won't Help Set Goals
Read Case Study: https://miro.com/templates/prune-the-product-tree/

# Description

If you're a product manager, customers and other stakeholders likely give you a lot of ideas for how to make your product better. But it may be hard to figure out which ideas you should actually use or if those ideas will work well with the product you already have.

If this is your problem, a product structure tree can help you figure out which new features to add first. The goal of the game is to let customers and members of the product team come up with ideas and add them to the template. You can then talk about the results and "prune" back the tree until you have a good picture of your product's future.

This game gives customers a sense of ownership over the next version of your product by letting them take part in making it. This method can not only build trust and loyalty with customers, but it can also show the product management team what customers want and need from the product.
