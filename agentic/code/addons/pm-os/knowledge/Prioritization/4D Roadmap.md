# 4D Roadmap

Use When: Vision Setting/Long-Term Roadmapping, Need Leadership Buy-In, Building a New Product
Author: Reforge
Don't Use When: In Delivery, Need Quick Decisions, Prioritising individual features
Best for Prioritising...: Initiatives/Epics, Themes/Lanes
Advantages: Creates Strategic Alignment, Forces Multi-Lens Thinking, Builds Leadership Buy-In
Limitations: Time Intensive, Requires Strong Strategic Context, Won't Help With Delivery

Read Case Study: https://www.reforge.com/previews/product/product-roadmaps

## Description

The 4D Product Roadmapping process is a strategic approach to roadmap prioritization that involves brainstorming initiatives from four different lenses: **Strategy**, **Vision**, **Customer**, and **Business**. This process creates leverage that single-lens frameworks don't, because it forces the team to pressure-test each initiative against multiple dimensions simultaneously.

The four lenses:

- **Strategy**: What moves are required by your competitive position or strategic intent?
- **Vision**: What does the long-term product vision demand be built?
- **Customer**: What problems are customers explicitly experiencing or requesting?
- **Business**: What initiatives improve key performance indicators and commercial outcomes?

Initiatives that score well across multiple lenses get priority. Single-lens items get questioned.

Most useful when you need to create a focused narrative for leadership to secure buy-in on a roadmap direction, rather than defend a backlog of features.
