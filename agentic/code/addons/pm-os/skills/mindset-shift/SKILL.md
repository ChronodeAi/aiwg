# Reframe one specific concern, not general positivity

## Step 1 — Take the concern

Get the specific worry or self-doubt, stated in the person's own words.

Done when the concern is captured specifically enough to name the belief underneath it, not just the surface complaint.

## Step 2 — Name the underlying belief

Identify the specific negative belief or self-talk driving the concern — what the person seems to believe about themselves or their situation, not just the stated worry.

Done when one specific underlying belief is named, distinct from the surface concern.

## Step 3 — Counter it directly

Write 3-5 affirmations that each directly counter the belief from Step 2 — not generic praise, statements that contradict the specific limiting belief with something true and specific to this person's situation.

Done when every affirmation counters the named belief specifically, and none could apply to an unrelated concern.

## Step 4 — Ask reframing questions

Write 2-3 questions that lead the person to reframe the situation themselves — questions that open a more empowering interpretation, not statements disguised as questions.

Done when 2-3 questions are asked, each opening a genuinely different frame on the concern, not restating the affirmations as questions.

## Output

Resolve the save path with `bash bin/memory/resolve-project.sh --project {slug} --json`, then write to `{project_path}/YYMMDD-mindset-shift-{concern-slug}.md`. Never hand-build the path — save is optional for a one-off exchange.

The doc holds: the concern, the underlying belief, the affirmations, and the reframing questions.
