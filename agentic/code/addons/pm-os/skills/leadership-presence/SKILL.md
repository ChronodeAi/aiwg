# Review your own meeting performance for leadership presence

## Step 1 — Take the transcript and your statements

Get the full meeting transcript and the specific statements that were yours.

Done when the transcript and your statements within it are both identified.

## Step 2 — Assess three dimensions

For influence (did your words and ideas move the room), clarity (concision, directness), and presence (projected authority and confidence): find specific moments in your statements that show the issue, not a general impression.

Done when each of the three dimensions has at least one cited moment from the transcript, not a generic assessment.

## Step 3 — Propose the adjustment

For each cited moment, propose the specific thing to say or do differently next time — not "be more assertive," but the actual rephrased line or structural change.

Done when every cited issue has a concrete, sayable adjustment, not an abstract trait to improve.

## Step 4 — Name the one priority

Of everything found, name the single adjustment that would matter most in the next meeting.

Done when one adjustment is named as the priority, distinct from the full list.

## Output

Resolve the save path with `bash bin/memory/resolve-project.sh --project {slug} --json`, then write to `{project_path}/YYMMDD-leadership-presence-{meeting-slug}.md`. Never hand-build the path.

The doc holds: the cited findings across all three dimensions with adjustments, and the one prioritized focus.
