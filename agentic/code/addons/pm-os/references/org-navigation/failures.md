# Failure Catalog — Anti-Patterns and Recovery

Layer 6 output. Quotes are Shreyas Doshi's exact phrasing unless attributed to Machiavelli/Greene.

## 1. Burning bridges while getting things done
- **Class:** emotional bias / coordination breakdown. **Recoverable until the ethical line.**
- **Root cause:** deep frustration in a high-friction environment pushing a PM to force outcomes.
- **Early warnings:** "always angry, always upset" and visibly so; being "inconsistent"; "bend the truth sometimes"; "two different things to two different people."
- **Consequence:** colleagues "feel hurt... feel like we've been disagreeable... aggressive."
- **Prevention/recovery:** hard ethical floor — "don't do it, absolutely don't do it." For perception repair, the uncompromising/open split. Owner: `respond-under-fire` branch E.

## 2. Steamrolling colleagues
- **Class:** coordination breakdown. **Recoverable.**
- **Root cause:** driving impact with no space for discussion: "we're doing it my way, I'm the PM, so too bad."
- **Consequence:** "people don't feel heard."
- **Prevention/recovery:** separate what from how; the blind-spots invitation. Owner: `respond-under-fire` E, pre-empted in `prep-the-room` step 5.

## 3. Neglecting optics work (optics debt)
- **Class:** over-optimization on craft / capability gap. **Slow-moving, hidden.**
- **Root cause:** ego-protective belief: "optics is distasteful and I should just do good work and let everything else take care of itself."
- **Consequence:** starved of resources and mandate: "that's how you get resources... the responsibility to be able to drive the impact."
- **Prevention/recovery:** belief audit + time-boxed block + reward pairing. Owner: `manage-the-upward-channel` branch 2.

## 4. Wasting political capital on truth-seeking
- **Class:** emotional bias (ego). **Recoverable; chronic form ends in burnout.**
- **Root cause:** "the dopamine hit of being right, of being smarter, of outwitting the other person."
- **Early warnings:** "can you believe how fucked up this place is"; moral/intellectual superiority posture.
- **Consequence:** "they think you're full of shit"; "immense frustration and disdain"; "you drive yourself completely insane and then burnout happens."
- **Prevention/recovery:** curious-influence reframe + LNO. Owner: `pick-your-battles` steps 1–2.

## 5. Mishandling launch date commitments
- **Class:** missing information / shared delusion. **Preventable, painful to recover.**
- **Root cause:** believing precise estimation exists; yielding to exec pressure ("they're getting pushed on the dates by the CEO so they need to provide something").
- **Early warnings:** hunting for the magic "spreadsheet or template."
- **Consequence:** "banging your head against the wall"; GTM blowback; "let's do a postmortem and all that nonsense — bullshit waste of time."
- **Prevention/recovery:** two-date system. Owner: `manage-the-upward-channel` branch 1.

## 6. Mishandling difficult people
- **Class:** misdiagnosis / weak evaluation. **Recoverable.**
- **Root cause:** assuming malice or defaulting to one diagnosis (e.g., always "misaligned incentives") when it may be personalities, information gaps, or abrasive-style-right-intent.
- **Early warnings:** repeatedly escalating "we need incentives to be aligned"; avoiding contact ("I don't want to feel this friction").
- **Consequence:** "the disagreeableness and disagreement continues forever."
- **Prevention/recovery:** root-cause first, then the elephant script. Owner: `respond-under-fire` C.

## 7. Escalating wrongly
- **Class:** poor sequencing / emotional bias. **Costly — trust destruction.**
- **Root cause:** defensive reaction to negative feedback; "we are sharp and we can argue really well" so we argue instead of owning.
- **Early warnings:** "yeah, because legal is a shit show... and this person is incompetent"; bypassing the counterpart straight to their VP.
- **Consequence:** counterpart "is not going to feel heard... next performance review it's going to be the same thing."
- **Prevention/recovery:** self-inquiry → direct repair conversation → coach for wording. Owner: `respond-under-fire` D; deliberate escalations only via `pick-your-battles`.

## 8. Drama participation
- **Class:** emotional bias. **Recoverable; chronic form is reputation-defining.**
- **Root cause:** operating as an adult baby; impulsively assuming malice.
- **Early warnings:** "this is a bad person, this is an uncaring person" thoughts; being soothed by venting.
- **Consequence:** "a lot of unnecessary drama"; absorbing "the garbage that goes on through so many minds."
- **Prevention/recovery:** neutral self-interrogation; long-run: "the more I ask why, the greater the likelihood that these impulsive thoughts don't even come." Owner: `respond-under-fire` step 0 + F.

## 9. The drip-fed strike (Machiavelli)
- **Class:** poor sequencing. **Often unrecoverable — permanent enemy created.**
- **Root cause:** timidity: escalating in fragments, partial reprimands, half-measures.
- **Consequence:** a wounded opponent who "can avenge themselves of lighter injuries"; you are "compelled to keep the knife in hand."
- **Prevention/recovery:** if a strike is justified at all, one complete prepared blow; if not justified, none. Owner: `pick-your-battles` step 5.

## 10. Habitual severity (Machiavelli/Greene)
- **Class:** over-optimization / enforcement addiction. **Unrecoverable at the limit — pariah status.**
- **Root cause:** reaching for the nuclear option on minor inconveniences.
- **Early warnings:** more than one battle per quarter; people pre-flinching in your meetings.
- **Consequence:** severities "badly employed... multiply with time rather than decrease"; universal hatred.
- **Prevention/recovery:** the overuse flag and quarterly battle counter. Owner: `pick-your-battles` overuse warnings.

## 11. Overplayed fox (Machiavelli/Greene)
- **Class:** incentive misalignment with own reputation. **Slow-moving, hidden until sudden.**
- **Root cause:** indirect moves (options control, thumbscrews, cat's-paws) used so often they become visible.
- **Consequence:** "caught being too cunning, you lose the trust required to lead cross-functional teams"; exposed thumbscrews provoke "vicious, irrational backlash."
- **Prevention/recovery:** default to demonstration (Law 9) over manipulation; indirect moves as exception, not habit. Owner: `pick-your-battles` step 5.
