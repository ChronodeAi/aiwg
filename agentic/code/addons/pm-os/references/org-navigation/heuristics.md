# Heuristics Catalog — Signals and Tells

Layer 4 output. What to observe, what it indicates, what response it triggers. Quotes are Shreyas Doshi's exact phrasing unless marked otherwise.

## Maturity tells (baby in disguise)

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| "Operating as just a victim of whatever circumstances there are"; "small things irritate them to a fairly large degree"; "unable to regulate their own emotions"; "excessively reliant on other people to soothe them"; seeks constant attention; "repeat these baby patterns over and over... without learning much" | A baby in disguise; on a team this means "a lot of unnecessary drama"; without rare intervention "this is their destiny" | "Discard judgment, discard superiority." Use as influence input; as team/company avoidance signal; as restructuring signal ("should no longer be on the team") |

## Culture tells (3 PM cultures)

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| Bottom-quartile PM wields outsized influence "mainly because of his or her title"; PM as "dictator"; eng says "we built what the PMs asked us to build" | PM Dominated | "Nudge it over time to PM guided"; invite eng pushback; co-write the PRD with the EM |
| Highly competent PM with "low... at best medium degree of influence"; eng or sales is "Top Dog"; "PM as housekeeping" | PM Serviced — "root cause of the vast majority" of cross-functional dysfunction | Set expectations; influence runs through the dominant function |
| High-competence PM = high influence, low-competence PM ≠ influence; PM as "tour guide" | PM Guided (healthy) | "Sustain... as long as possible"; accept unhappiness as normal |

## Friction tells

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| No friction at all; lane-policing; title arguments; "what will make my function's life easier"; "every single engineer needs to be happy otherwise PM is not doing their job" | Bad friction; dysfunctional subculture (happiness-driven or promotion-driven eng, virtue-driven design) | "Call out the elephant in the room"; shed "the fantasy of being happy all the time" |
| Tension between PM and eng *on what to build* | Good friction | Protect it; do not smooth it over |

## Excuse tells (reasons are BS)

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| "Beautiful reasons — well, this happened, and then there was this problem in this service... a certain race condition"; blame-shifts ("this team did not convey to us...") | Smart person producing "really amazing excuses" instead of "radical complete ownership" | File as BS; "fine, there is a reason, let's move on — what can we do now" |

## Bridge-burning tells (self-monitoring)

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| "Always angry, always upset" and visibly so; bending the truth; "two different things to two different people" | Ethical line approaching | "Don't do it, absolutely don't do it" — hard stop |
| Colleagues seem hurt; "people don't feel heard, they feel like this person is steamrolling over them" | Aggression perception building | The uncompromising/open split + blind-spots invitation |

## Optics-debt tells (self-monitoring)

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| Belief that "optics is distasteful and I should just do good work"; procrastinating on status updates; avoiding the spotlight | Optics debt accruing; leverage shrinking ("that's how you get resources") | Underlying-belief audit, then the time-boxed optics block |

## Exec tells

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| Imposed "shit planning process" / "horrible planning template" with zero value | Incompetent / optics-obsessed exec — "you cannot solve it" | Inside/outside world split (shit umbrella) |

## Power tells (Machiavelli / Greene)

| Observe | Indicates | Trigger response |
|---|---|---|
| Your win threatens someone's status; process objections appear from nowhere | Snare (fox read) | Pre-empt: attribute, share credit, or control the options |
| A provocation that flatters your ego to engage | Bait — engaging "lowers your status to their level" | Law 36: disdain; monitor monthly for genuine growth |
| Your "trivial annoyance" keeps gaining allies and surface area | Misclassified growing threat | Reclassify; route to pick-your-battles |
| You crave the "dopamine hit of being right" | Truth-seeker ego, not leverage | LNO test before any spend |
