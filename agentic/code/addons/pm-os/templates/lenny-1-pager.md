# Lenny's 1-Pager

Author: Lenny Rachitsky
Source: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/my-favorite-templates-issue-37
Also available as: https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence/templates/lennys-product-requirements
Use When: Quick Alignment, Discovery, Feature Work
Don't Use When: Build, Exec Buy-In
Best For: Stakeholder Alignment, Problem Framing
Audience: Direct Team, Cross-Functional
Detail Level: Light
Time to Complete: 30 minutes
Artefact: Document
Advantages: Ultra-concise, problem-first, fast to produce, works for any project size
Limitations: Too light for eng handoff, no technical detail, no rollout plan

---

*"Nailing the problem statement is the single most important step in solving any problem." — Lenny Rachitsky*

*This template follows Lenny's three-step framework: (1) Crystallize the problem, (2) Align with your team, (3) Keep coming back to the problem.*
