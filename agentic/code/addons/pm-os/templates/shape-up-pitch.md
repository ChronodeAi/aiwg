# Shape Up Pitch

Author: Ryan Singer
Source: https://basecamp.com/shapeup/webbook (Chapters 5-6)
Use When: Shaping, Feature Work, Team Autonomy
Don't Use When: Exec Buy-In, Build
Best For: Scope Definition
Audience: Cross-Functional
Detail Level: Medium
Time to Complete: 1-2 hours
Artefact: Document
Advantages: Prevents scope creep, empowers teams, keeps solutions at right abstraction level, introduces appetite as constraint
Limitations: Too loose for eng handoff, no detailed specs, assumes team autonomy

---

*"A pitch defines the key elements of a solution at the right level of abstraction — specific enough to make progress, rough enough that the team has room to work out the details." — Ryan Singer*

*Shape Up pitches deliberately stay away from detailed specs. The solution is described with "fat marker sketches" — broad strokes that define boundaries without filling in implementation details.*
